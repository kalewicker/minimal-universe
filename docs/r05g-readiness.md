# R05g read-only readiness and hypothesis review

This stage consolidates the public R05b–R05f evidence. Its own source-bound
registration precedes execution. It reads public source, protocols and receipts;
it does not open bulk trajectories, rerun calibration, sample nulls, evolve a
graph, execute native binaries, evaluate candidate statistics or open holdouts.

## Registered review

Reconstruct every one of the 969 formal rules crossed with all 14 engineering
seeds at the unchanged 50-event horizon. Compare R05d's closed-form projection
with its separately written incremental integer arithmetic, and compare the
reciprocal-pair budget bound with direct counting of the available unordered
vertex pairs. Every unavailable cell remains in the denominator. Retained
vertices include isolates; size-compatible projections do not prove reachability.
No shorter horizon or favorable subgrammar is selected.

For N retained vertices and E edges, reciprocal density cannot exceed
`min(floor(E/2), choose(N,2))/choose(N,2)` when N >= 2. Subtracting the
nonnegative conditional degree-null mean cannot increase this ceiling. A ceiling
strictly below 0.05 excludes that proposed positive endpoint contrast; equality
does not. Undefined density is separate from zero. A cell not excluded by the
ceiling is untested and has no candidate status. This bound is not a bound on
the paired difference-in-changes intervention effect, and it does not reject
every possible G2 mechanism or hypothesis.

Validate the saved R05f query identities, histogram/interval accounting, root
custody and coverage labels without regenerating the draws. Preserve all 56
slots, all 32 known-mean coverage checks, the large-query invariant-only scope,
and sampled constants as unresolved. The calibration quota of 56 and its radius
must not become an unregistered campaign quota or parent-level confidence bound.
Source-map checks bind the prior public evidence; they are not new experimental
replay or independent certification of every historical result.

The review produces an exclusive full-cell ledger, deterministic receipt and
failure-preserving execution record, within 120 seconds and 64 MiB. Its
engineering acceptance requires agreement with all existing R05d/R05e counts,
the full conditional observer size domain, and the saved R05f accounting. A
successful review may conclude `not_launch_ready`; review correctness and
scientific readiness are different outcomes.

## Decision framework

Record separately the calibrated reference kernel, calibrated supplied-state
observer, conditional size compatibility, proposed-contrast ceiling, unknown
reachability, undefined densities, motif exclusions, null informativeness, and
unregistered scientific design requirements. The R05c exact survival calculation
still has a three-event domain. R05f observed identity and local avoidance do not
provide a 50-event unconditional survival distribution; persistence is descriptive
in the frozen contract and is not made a new promotion gate by this review.

No launch follows automatically from calibration. A production design still needs
its structural estimand and family, parent registry and inherited splits,
replication/power, target/sham construction and scheduler coupling, estimators,
resampling/test families, null-query quota, and measured world-run/storage budget.
The frozen minimum of 30 parents is not a power calculation. Runtime of synthetic
null calibration is not throughput of a scientific campaign. This review records
these as unestablished in the reviewed registrations rather than as measured
failures or evidence of zero effects.

The useful next design step is a separately registered observable/hypothesis
design review. Before any new trajectories, state the physical question, identify
which effects are erased by size normalization or aliasing, and analyze proposed
observables on synthetic positive, negative, null-degenerate and unavailable
controls. Require an explicit size-scaling analysis and an intervention estimand
before freezing any replacement. Do not pick an observable because it makes an
existing law, seed or saved trajectory look favorable. A replacement would be a
new hypothesis/version; R02 thresholds, consumed registrations and negative
readiness results remain historical evidence. This stage neither chooses nor
implements a replacement metric and authorizes no R06 execution.

Keeping the existing contrast is also a valid scientific choice if a later
registration explicitly studies its limited or negative outcomes and justifies
the cost. The ceiling does not prohibit such research. It does remove the basis
for treating more runtime or better Monte Carlo precision as a repair of the
fresh-node endpoint contrast.
