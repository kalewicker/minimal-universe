hashes. Independent Python replay matched all edge IDs, allocator counters, world
event producers, consumed occurrences, branch RNG states and histories. The matrix
covers 220 external contexts, four orientations, three schedulers and two parent

Seed distinctness does not establish statistical independence.

and advances the existing allocator. A separate intervention record identifies
external births and their consumed provenance; fresh intervention edges have no
world-event producer. Causal ancestry analysis must include this record, rather
than treating ordinary event-parent links as a complete intervention-aware DAG.
