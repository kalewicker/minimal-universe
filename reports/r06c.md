# R06c scientific design and feasibility review

**Static engineering review passed; scientific status remains untested.**
[Design and limitations](../docs/r06c-scientific-design.md),
[source-bound registration](../protocols/r06c.json), and
[deterministic receipt](r06c.json) preserve the complete declared scope.

The 136-bag deletion census found two degree-matched endpoint multisets. Only the
original reciprocal pair also matched loop count. With unique target occurrences,
there is no distinct sham satisfying those strict deletion controls.

The 24-permutation rewiring census found 15 looped permutations, five that retain
old arcs, two directed four-cycles and two alternate reciprocal pairings. The four
eligible target/sham combinations passed all **880 checks across 220 fixed external
contexts**. Degrees, loops, eligible-match counts and participation of retained
outside occurrences agree. Independent pair enumeration verified match formulas;
an independent scorer agreed with R06b raw scores. Initial sham-minus-target score
is exactly 4/E. Newly emitted participation profiles are retained in the local
ledger and need not agree. This is limited static matching, not general sham validity.

The proposed paired outcome measures subsequent change after intervention. It does
not include the instantaneous offset, isolate a local causal mechanism or establish
recovery. Native allocation/provenance and branch RNG behavior are not implemented.
Parent namespace, inherited splits, population, eligibility, missingness and scheduler
coupling are explicitly design proposals, not an admitted production registry.

Nine conservative power scenarios and 27 work scenarios are preserved in the
receipt. Using the bounded independent-outcome inequality of
[Hoeffding (1963)](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830), the single-test
0.05-gap scenario requires 64,000 independent, fully observed parent groups;
its two 50-event branches imply 6,400,000 conditional event targets, excluding
parent generation. This sufficient bound is deliberately conservative, not an
estimated optimal sample size or an adoption of the 0.05 threshold.

Raw-score inference needs **zero degree-null samples**. If four optional null
queries were requested per parent in that scenario, the planned quota would be
256,000 queries and up to 4,194,304,000 draws. Simultaneous MC error bounds use
the entire prospective quota, never the previous calibration quota. No campaign
runtime, availability, independence, storage budget or feasibility is established.

The registered attempt used 0.0864 seconds (rounded upward) and 183,001 bytes before
its execution receipt, within 120 seconds and 64 MiB. There were **zero world
transitions and zero null draws**. All **126 Python tests passed**. A pre-registration
unit-test import-path error was fixed before the protocol was frozen; no registered
attempt failed or was replaced. Clean public-source replay is recorded in custody.
