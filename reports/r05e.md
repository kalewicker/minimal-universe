# R05e long-horizon observer design closeout

Date: 2026-10-24. Static protocol: `R05e-v1`. Completed attempt: `r05e-002`.
Design audit: **pass**. Implementation/calibration: **not executed**.
Scientific status: **untested**. R05d's campaign-readiness gate remains unfulfilled.

The [new observer design](../docs/r05e-long-observer.md) and
[machine-readable plan](../protocols/r05e-observer-design.json) specify larger-state
local exposure, exact-versus-sampled degree-null dispatch, outward Monte Carlo
uncertainty, unresolved sampled constants and observed occurrence-identity
tracking. They retain terminal/censored/unsupported endpoints and distinguish
realised identity indicators from exact full-history survival prediction.
No production observer or null sampler was implemented in this milestone.

## Geometric limitation beyond the software domains

For the proposed contrast `observed reciprocal density - degree-null mean`, the
null mean is nonnegative. With V vertices and E edge occurrences, the contrast
is therefore bounded above by
`min(floor(E/2),choose(V,2))/choose(V,2)` when V>=2. Each reciprocal pair requires
two opposite directed occurrences; loops and extra parallel edges cannot improve
that ceiling. This is a mathematical bound for this contrast, not an observed
effect or a retroactive interpretation of every previously supplied statistic.

The full 969-rule, 14-seed event-50 panel gives:

| Classification | Cells |
| --- | ---: |
| No initial match: event 50 unavailable | 3,876 |
| Insufficient edge budget: event 50 unavailable | 170 |
| Conditional endpoint density undefined | 420 |
| Proposed contrast's upper bound strictly below 0.05 | 7,420 |
| Not excluded by that upper bound | 1,680 |
| Full panel | 13,566 |

All 7,420 fresh-node cells not already excluded by necessary availability tests
fall below the frozen structural threshold at event 50. They would have at least
51 vertices and at most 54 edges; even the generous bound `27/choose(51,2)` is
less than 0.05. Expanding measurement software cannot make this contrast exceed
its geometric ceiling. The 1,680 non-excluded cells are not candidates or proved
reachable trajectories, and have no measured positive effect.

This does not rule out organization under other measurements. A different scalar,
normalization, threshold or hypothesis needs a separate registered decision.
None is changed here, and no favorable subgrammar is selected as a repair.

## Proposed metrology and uncertainty

The proposed 64-node/64-edge/4,032-match observer domain covers the size projections
of all 9,520 cells not ruled out by initial eligibility/count conditions. This is
design coverage only; no new domain is implemented or calibrated yet.

For at most eight edges the design retains exact target-stub enumeration. Larger
states use a fixed 16,384 draws, labelled Fisher–Yates target permutations and a
separate SHA-256 counter stream. A frozen quota counts every planned query slot.
The 0.01 family Monte Carlo error budget, ideal-iid model, rational outward radius,
word-budget failure and constant-sample status are explicit. The underlying
bounded-independent-variable inequality is
[Hoeffding's result](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830); the method
contract gives the application, union-bound derivation and exact conservative
rounding. This is not a world-parent confidence interval, p-value, proof of iid
hash output, or automatic combined 95% scientific coverage.

At 16,384 draws, the conservative certified mean-error radii are:

| Frozen query quota | Radius | Sufficient draws for radius at most 0.01 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.015625 | 40,000 |
| 1,000 | 0.023438 | 90,000 |
| 1,000,000 | 0.029232 | 140,000 |

These are design arithmetic, not empirical precision or throughput. They use a
deliberately conservative dyadic certificate, not the optimal logarithmic radius.
No sample count is increased after results. The [precision CSV](r05e-precision.csv)
includes all nine fixed draw-count/quota scenarios.

A rare-support fixture has 32 edges in each direction between two vertices.
A degree-preserving all-loop target permutation has score zero, whereas the
original has score one; the zero-score arrangement has probability
`1/1,832,624,140,942,590,534`. A finite sample can thus be constant despite truly
nonconstant support. The proposal returns `sampled_constant_unresolved` rather
than declaring degeneracy from such samples. These are static support witnesses;
no random null draws were made.

## Audited design and future calibration matrix

The static audit checked 46,234 labelled shuffle-choice vectors for lengths zero
through eight against the complete permutation sets, nine certified precision
scenarios against an independent rational search, and all 13,566 rule/seed cells
against independently counted size and reciprocal-pair budgets.
All 77 Python tests passed, including the new JSON-fixture regression described
below. No native runs, graph transitions, null samples or holdout reads occurred.

The proposed R05f calibration freezes eight small known-null graphs, six large
boundary/control graphs, four root keys and 56 query slots: 917,504 planned score
evaluations. It includes exact small-null distributions, degree preservation,
independent scoring, exposure compatibility, RNG/rejection/replay, identity and
unavailable-endpoint controls. All 32 small sampled intervals must cover their
exact means under the full quota; a miss consumes that gate and cannot be repaired
by seed replacement or more draws. The gate can fail by chance. No R05f query has
been executed or authorized by this design audit.

## Preserved failure and source-only repair

`r05e-001` stopped on a software error: JSON endpoint lists were used directly as
set members in the rare-support check. Its [failure receipt](r05e-001-failure.json),
[custody](r05e-001-custody.json), full local trace and exact source snapshot are
preserved. The original source is in commit
[`6462d97`](https://github.com/kalewicker/minimal-universe/commit/6462d97).

The [repair registration](../protocols/r05e-repair.json) pins the failure and fixed
source, normalizes endpoint lists to tuples and adds a regression using the actual
JSON fixture. The unchanged complete audit ran as `r05e-002` and passed in 1.20
seconds. Its ledger hash is identical to the failed attempt's ledger, confirming
that no matrix inputs, seeds, draw counts, thresholds or computed ledger rows were
changed. The failed attempt is not represented as passing.
