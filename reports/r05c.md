## Diagnostic controls

- Two parallel loop occurrences have two eligible ordered matches, both consuming
  both occurrences. A one-occurrence target has one-step avoidance probability
  zero. Substituting the G1 count-only formula would give 1/2 and is invalid here.
- In the weighted four-edge fixture, two target occurrences have participation
  counts summing to six, but only five distinct ordered matches exist and all five
  hit the target union. Exposure counts the union and never reports a negative
  avoidance probability from double counting.
- With the registered partial-horizon control, joint reach-and-survive probability
  at event two is 1/2, unavailable probability is 1/2, and conditional survival
  among reached branches is one. At event three every branch is unavailable;
  conditional survival is undefined. No terminal state is padded forward.
- A nonparticipating occurrence survives the first event with probability one,
  but the event-two horizon can still be unavailable. Absence of exposure is not
  proof that a later requested event exists.

## Limits and next boundary

The exact model is bounded to eight nodes, eight edges and a three-event horizon.
It is calibrated for uniform ordered-match selection; deterministic schedulers
need their own interpretation. It does not estimate long-horizon sampling error,
validate a target/sham construction, identify causal effects, compute statistical
confidence intervals, or satisfy the unchanged 50-event intervention gate.
R02 motif multiplicity exclusions and exact-null limits remain unchanged.

Next is R05d: a separately registered static campaign-feasibility and measurement
domain review over the declared grammar and seeds. It must reconcile intended
horizons with execution, null, motif and exposure domains before budget pilots or
discovery. This closeout does not authorize a campaign, candidate promotion or
holdout use. All 16,774 reserved G1 jobs remain unopened.
