## Limits and next milestone

These results validate bounded structural measurements, exact mechanical
persistence, null distributions, and synthetic paired arithmetic. They do not
validate a detector of regeneration, establish statistical power, or demonstrate
causal organization. The gate accepts supplied statistics/flags rather than
verifying data custody itself. Bootstrap computation, a valid prespecified test,
real intervention machinery, and scientific inference remain unimplemented.

Next is a **separately registered R03 baseline design and feasibility check**.
The current exact degree null stops at eight edges and motif measurement accepts
only arc multiplicities up to two, so a large growth census cannot silently use
these as universally applicable controls. R03 must choose an honest supported
matrix or register a new null-calibration extension first, freeze parent keys,
resource limits and outcome accounting, then launch only its declared scope.
The current calibration does not authorize that launch.
