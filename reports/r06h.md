## Cost of verification and evidence retention

Independent Python replay took **120.388 of 154.349 seconds** (78.0%). The native
processes used 32.679 seconds, including 5.447 seconds of evolution and 15.994
seconds of checkpoint verification. The saved timings support these run-wide
totals. They leave per-branch replay costs and the breakdown of native process
overhead unresolved.

Native JSONL histories accounted for **284,045,954 of 301,571,950 retained bytes**
(94.2%, before the final execution receipt). Of those history bytes, 259,750,136
came from the three-arc rule batches. The compact ledger, inputs, and timing and
administrative records account for the remainder. The machine-specific costs
describe the recorded run. Any future reduction in trace size should preserve
exact history reconstruction and independent verification.
