# R06b — edge-budget reference observer and synthetic calibration

**Engineering/calibration status: pass. Production metric adoption: none.
Scientific status: untested.**

The separate [versioned reference observer](../docs/r06b-observer.md) implements
`mu-g2-edge-budget-v1`: Q=2R/E, with distinct reciprocal nonloop vertex pairs R
and all edge occurrences E, including loops and duplicates. It accepts supplied
graphs with at most 64 retained nodes and 64 occurrences. Empty graphs remain
undefined; nonempty aliased loops have score zero. This new reference module
changes no R02 metric, threshold, motif policy or consumed receipt.

The [protocol](../protocols/r06b.json),
[fixed calibration design](../protocols/r06b-calibration-design.json) and source
hashes were registered before the exclusive `r06b-001` launch. The full matrix

The family quota is **892 = 816 + 76**. It counts exact, undefined and repeated
fixture requests rather than only sampled nonempty requests. The fixed sample
count is 16,384 and the Monte Carlo family failure budget is 1/100. The certified
millionth-grid radius is **0.023438** (11,719/500,000), checked independently with
exact arithmetic. The largest known-mean error was 173/16,384 (about 0.01056).
No seed replacement, additional samples, omitted slots or changed criteria were
used. This calibration quota is not a campaign quota.

Every sampled labelled permutation matched a separate direct-SHA replay. Both
degree sequences and the occurrence count were conserved, every score matched
independent adjacency logic, and all 21,889,024 attempted words were accounted
for. Exact distributions agreed with the R06a matrix oracle and an independently
structured multiplicity-weighted endpoint-sequence enumeration. The new sampler
uses the metric-specific `mu-g2-budget-null-word-v1` header while reusing frozen
R05f rejection, accounting and shuffle primitives. Prior source maps remained
unchanged before and after execution.

The exact-control requests returned 420 informative nulls, 390 degenerate nulls
and six undefined empty-graph outcomes. The sampled matrix returned 40
`witnessed_nonconstant` and 28 `sampled_constant_unresolved` samples, plus its eight
undefined slots. The four rare-support samples remained unresolved constants.
For that balanced 64-edge control, only the all-loop target assignment has score
zero; all others have score 1/32. Its analytic mean is
`(1-1/choose(64,32))/32`. This independent support proof supplies calibration truth
without changing what a finite constant sample establishes. Large slots outside
this declared analytic control have no unknown-mean coverage claim.

Exact small nulls, sampled larger nulls, undefined empty requests and RNG
exhaustion have distinct outcomes. An incomplete sampled query has partial
diagnostics and no mean or interval. Rational clipped mean intervals and reversed
contrast intervals concern Monte Carlo error under an ideal iid uniform
stub-permutation model. Deterministic SHA reproducibility is not a proof of iid
randomness, parent-level confidence, a scientific alpha or evidence of an effect.
Fixture instances include intentional repeated graphs and are not independent
scientific parents or a sample of G2 law performance.

The synthetic paired bookkeeper has its own `mu/G2-budget-cal-v1/` namespace,
score fields, inherited parent partitions, scheduler and successful-event horizon
metadata. It rejects duplicate parents, mismatched branch identities, split or
scheduler changes, invalid scores and invented unavailable endpoints. It
preserves initial/endpoint match counts and eligibility differences. Any missing
pair prevents an aggregate mean while retaining every parent. Equal match counts
do not validate a sham. The draft outcome measures subsequent differential score
change over at most 50 successful events and excludes instantaneous score change;
it does not identify a local causal effect or validate a scientific estimator.
