Date: 2026-10-24. Protocol: `R05b-v1`. Engineering validation: **pass**.

## Boundary and failure checks

All 45 Python tests passed. Seven new oracle tests cover parent deduplication and
merging, atomic cap rejection, singleton-match RNG consumption, word wrap and
rejection, alias-induced parallel output, immutable prefix snapshots, and complete
matrix size. Native CTest passed in both release and sanitizer builds, including
cap/overflow boundaries, stable IDs, two-parent ancestry, checkpoint corruption
and cross-version rejection, and allocation-failure injection after temporary edge
and history preparation. Checked-add overflow is tested directly because hard
reference limits make near-uint64 graph counters unreachable through the CLI.

A [CLI smoke receipt](r05b-cli.json) records that the example runs for six events,
resumes to the identical final state, and refuses to overwrite its checkpoint
without changing saved bytes. Expected rejection tests are negative controls,
not failed matrix attempts. Prior R05a/R04a public receipt/source checks passed.
