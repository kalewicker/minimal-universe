```sh
python3 -B scripts/audit_r04.py --verify reports/r04a.json
```

Writing a new receipt requires a new output path; the checker refuses overwrite.
No command in this checker launches a simulation or opens a holdout trajectory.

R05 is now the next roadmap milestone: propose and formally specify a two-edge
left-hand side as a new experiment. Its design must address distinct edge
occurrences, vertex aliasing, overlapping matches, transactional consumption,
event ancestry, and scheduler semantics before a reference kernel or campaign.
Those choices require new controls and calibration; the one-edge persistence
formula and commutation theorem cannot simply be assumed for the extension.
This closeout neither implements nor authorizes an R05 campaign, and does not
establish that a two-edge grammar will produce organization.
